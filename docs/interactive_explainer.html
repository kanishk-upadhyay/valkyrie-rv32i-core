⚙️ Microarchitecture Component Guide

Data Forwarding Unit
forwarding_unit.v

Eliminates RAW dependencies without stalls. Multiplexes intermediate results from EX/MEM (priority 1) or MEM/WB (priority 2) directly into ALU inputs.

Hazard Detection Unit
hazard_unit.v

Detects Load-Use conditions when a load destination register matches decode stage source operands. Injects 1-cycle bubble into ID/EX and freezes PC & IF/ID.

Machine-Mode CSR Unit
csr_unit.v

Implements RISC-V Privileged Architecture v1.12. Supports mstatus, mie, mip, mtvec, mepc, mcause, and mscratch with 1-cycle trap vectoring and mret return.

Tactical Bus Adapter
memory_bus_adapter.v

Integrates unified 64KB SRAM, military UART streaming console (0x10000000), 64-bit real-time hardware cycle timer (0x20000000), and Wishbone master expansion.
